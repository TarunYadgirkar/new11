# Under Cal — a 3D atlas of what lies beneath UC Berkeley

An interactive three.js reconstruction of UC Berkeley's underground: the steam
tunnel network and cogeneration plant, the planned Clean Energy Campus loop, the
buried Gardner Main Stacks, Strawberry Creek's culverts (the "Little Inch",
"Big Inch" and the city culvert under Allston Way), the Hayward Fault through
Memorial Stadium, the Lawson Adit, the Haviland Hall and Byerly seismograph
vaults, and the Downtown Berkeley BART station.

Open `index.html` in a modern browser (it loads three.js from jsDelivr).

## What you can do

- **Orbit / zoom / pan** the model, including from below the ground.
- **X-ray ground** – fade the terrain to see what is underneath.
- **Depth stretch** – exaggerate depths (1–10×) so shallow tunnels are readable.
- **Cross-section** – slice the ground east–west and see the cut face.
- **Fault slip** – shear Memorial Stadium along the Hayward Fault up to its 6 ft design offset (drawn 10× larger).
- **Walk it** – ride first-person through the steam tunnels (the 1999 Campanile → VLSB route), the Gardner Stacks, the Little Inch culvert, the city culvert, the Lawson Adit, the Byerly Vault and the BART subway.
- **Tour** – an 8-stop guided walkthrough.
- Click any feature for a record card with real dimensions, dates and cited sources.

## Accuracy

Every feature carries a confidence tag (Documented / Approximate / Schematic /
Planned / Historic). UC Berkeley does not publish as-built tunnel maps, so steam
tunnel corridors connect the documented points along plausible routes. Ground
relief is a simplified model. Do not use this for navigation or entry — the
tunnels are off-limits and dangerous. Sources are listed in the app under
"Sources & accuracy".
