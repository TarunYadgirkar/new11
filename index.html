How this model was built

Sources & accuracy

Every feature is placed from published descriptions: campus news archives, the Strawberry Creek management plan, the Berkeley Seismological Laboratory station book, UC Libraries, the Clean Energy Campus project and engineering press on the stadium retrofit. UC Berkeley does not publish as-built maps of its steam tunnels, so those corridors connect the documented points along plausible routes. Do not use this model for navigation, excavation or entry. The tunnels are off-limits and dangerous.

Model conventions

Horizontal positions are in metres from the base of Sather Tower (37.8721° N, 122.2578° W). Ground relief is a simplified model fitted to a few known elevations, such as the stadium field at about 410 ft. Depths below ground can be stretched with the Depth stretch slider so shallow tunnels are readable; the record card always shows real figures. Fault slip is drawn ten times larger than the slider value.
